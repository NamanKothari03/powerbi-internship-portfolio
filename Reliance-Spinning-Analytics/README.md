# Reliance Industries — Spinning Plant Analytics

## Overview

During my Data Analyst internship at Reliance Industries Limited, Naroda, I worked on Power BI dashboards and analytics projects related to textile manufacturing and spinning plant operations.

The work involved analyzing operational metrics, identifying opportunities for process improvement and exploring AI applications in manufacturing.

## Work Performed

### Power BI Dashboards

- Developed Power BI dashboards for the spinning plant to monitor production, efficiency and operational metrics.
- Created interactive visualizations for KPI reporting and performance analysis.
- Performed data cleaning, transformation and modeling to prepare operational data for analysis.
- Converted operational data into meaningful visual insights for business analysis.

### AI Integration

- Identified potential AI opportunities in spinning and mending processes.
- Explored applications of AI for process monitoring and optimization.

### Process Mapping

- Created Visio process maps of operational workflows.
- Developed workflow representations to support chatbot implementation and process improvement.
